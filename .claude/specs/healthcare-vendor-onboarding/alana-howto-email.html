Hi Alana,

Your new Vendor Onboarding portal is ready. It replaces the 17-page printed AFA vendor pack with a guided, mostly auto-filled flow — and it emails the signed Service Provider Network Agreement straight to you and Medu.

Getting in

  1. Open omni (omni.alphadirect.co.bw) and sign in with your normal @alphadirect.co.bw Microsoft login — no separate password.
  2. Go to Healthcare → Vendor Onboarding. Access is restricted to you.

Onboarding a vendor (about 5 minutes)

  1. Snap the documents. Photograph or upload the vendor's Certificate of Incorporation / company extract. The system reads them and pre-fills the fields for you.
  2. Confirm every field. Anything the system filled shows in an orange "please confirm" state. Check each one and tap Confirm — nothing is saved until you do. This is the safety net: the reader assists, you decide.
  3. Banking is typed by hand (never read from a document) and entered twice to catch typos.
  4. The AFA agreement. The full Service Provider Network Agreement is shown on screen; the vendor's details fill in automatically. The vendor accepts and signs on the device.
  5. Submit. That's it.

What happens on submit

  • A clean, branded signed agreement PDF is generated (print-ready).
  • It is emailed automatically to you (ankete@) and Medu (mtlagae@) for AFA counter-signature — EXCO is copied.
  • The vendor record is saved with status Pending review.

Printing a signed agreement

Open the PDF from the email (or the record) and print — it is laid out on AFA letterhead with the signature block and page numbers, ready to file.

A short walkthrough can be arranged any time. If a field ever looks wrong after the document is read, just edit it before confirming — you are always the final check.
